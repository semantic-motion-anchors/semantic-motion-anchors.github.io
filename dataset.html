How SEMANTIX was constructed

Annotators record which hands are involved, their shape and orientation, where they are and the path they follow. Each description ends with a short phrase explaining what the gesture communicates in context.

  1. Select gesture regions

    Start from previously annotated gesture regions in 10 TED Expressive talks and their transcripts.

  2. Review the first 231

    A primary annotator describes the first 231 of the 778 TED clips. A second expert independently reviews each description and accepts or revises it.

  3. Complete the reference set

    Using the finalized guidelines, the primary annotator completes the remaining TED clips. The same schema is used for 100 BEAT2 clips.

Automatic motion anchor construction

The automatic pipeline generates anchors for training; the human annotations above provide a reference for evaluation.

  1. Tokenize motion

    Discretize 3D gesture motion into separate body and hand primitives.

  2. Describe each primitive

    Reconstruct eight-frame primitives and describe arm position, hand shape, palm orientation and motion using rules.

  3. Assemble a narrative

    Combine the ordered primitive descriptions into a physical narrative for the gesture.

  4. Ground it in speech

    An LLM reads the motion narrative and transcript. It identifies the active hand or hands, describes the movement, infers its intent and checks that all three are consistent.

TED · Contrast

“a shorter life that's pain free or a longer life that”

Transcript

want a shorter life that's pain free or a longer life that might have a great deal of pain

Expert description

Physical formCommunicative intent

Both hands move synchronously to one side of the body with palms open, facing outward and slightly downward. The hands then move to the center of the body and separate horizontally, with one hand extending to other side as far as possible while palms face each other. This motion visually maps the contrast between short and long concepts to contrast between two cases or situations with emphasis.

TED · Symbolise

“streaming down both her cheeks”

Transcript

streaming down both

Gesture words: streaming down both her cheeks

Expert description

Physical formCommunicative intent

Both hands rise to face level with index fingers extended and other fingers closed, palms facing inward. The index fingers then trace straight downward from the cheeks to chest level, ending with the fingers pointing toward each other to convey symbolise tears down face.
